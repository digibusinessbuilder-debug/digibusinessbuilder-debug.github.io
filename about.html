OUR TEAM

Meet the Avicenna Care Team

Many people with autoimmune conditions such as lupus, Hashimoto’s, rheumatoid arthritis and psoriasis are told their symptoms are just part of daily life. At Avicenna Functional Medicine, we take a functional medicine approach to better understand the underlying contributors to these issues. Using integrative and evidence-based strategies, our team works with you to identify root causes and work toward your health goals. Avicenna Functional Medicine was formerly known as AIM Microbiome.

Founders

Fawad Ahmed, MD

Co-Founder and Chief Medical Officer

Dr. Fawad Ahmed is a board-certified nephrologist, educator, and CMO of Avicenna Functional Medicine. He earned his medical degree from the University of Florida College of Medicine, served as Chief Resident at Orlando Regional Medical Center, and completed his nephrology fellowship at the University of Rochester School of Medicine. With over two decades in practice, Dr. Ahmed began noticing a consistent link between autoimmune and metabolic conditions — what he calls the "AIM conditions" — and poor gut health. This observation led him and his wife, Sonia Mirza, APRN, to launch Avicenna Functional Medicine, a virtual functional medicine practice focused on finding the root causes of chronic symptoms. Dr. Ahmed is guided by the belief that "The road to better health starts in the kitchen, not the pharmacy," and often reminds patients that "Metabolic syndrome is a foodborne illness." Known for his compassionate, thorough care, he continues to lead a growing shift toward functional medicine — helping patients understand and address the factors behind their symptoms. Along with Sonia, he develops the treatment protocols for the practice.

Sonia Mirza, APRN, FNP-BC

Co-Founder & Director of Clinical Operations

Sonia Mirza is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner specializing in gut microbiome health. She has completed Gut Health and the Microbiome, an online course from Stanford University, and with her own experience of autoimmune illness, Sonia brings both clinical insight and lived experience to her care. As Co-Founder and Director of Clinical Operations, she develops and oversees personalized treatment protocols rooted in functional medicine principles. Sonia is committed to care that combines evidence-based medicine with empathy, education and partnership. She leads the practice's clinical operations and works alongside each patient's other clinicians.

Care team

Holly Matura

Health Coach (FDN-P)

Holly Matura is a health coach certified as a Functional Diagnostic Nutrition Practitioner. Working under the supervision of Dr. Fawad Ahmed for clients in Florida, and Sonia Mirza, APRN, for clients in Texas, she helps clients follow the plan their clinician sets: explaining recommendations, building habits around food, sleep and stress, and staying on track between visits. Diagnosis, lab interpretation and treatment decisions stay with the licensed clinicians.

Advisors

Our advisors share their expertise with the Avicenna team. They don't see patients through Avicenna.

Aneshia Barr, MSN-ED, RN

Patient Education and Outreach, Advisor

Aneshia Barr is a registered nurse with over 16 years of experience in healthcare, including 7 years in nursing education. With a deep passion for holistic and integrative health, she has a special interest in supporting people with autoimmune conditions, particularly lupus. A proud Floridian, Aneshia believes good care looks after the mind and spirit as well as the body. Drawing on her own experience of this functional medicine approach, Aneshia became an advocate for its root-cause philosophy. She works to raise awareness about the connection between gut health, lifestyle, and chronic disease, and advises the Avicenna team on patient education and outreach.

Wanita Patterson, APRN, FNP-C

Nurse Practitioner, Advisor

Wanita Patterson is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner with advanced training in functional and integrative medicine and a strong foundation in conventional care. Her clinical interests include hormone balance, metabolic health and gut health, and she believes in personalized, evidence-based care. Wanita advises the Avicenna team on integrative care.

Kirti Kalidas, MD, ND

Integrative Medicine Physician, Advisor

Dr. Kirti Kalidas is a board-certified Internal Medicine physician and licensed Naturopathic Doctor with over two decades of experience. After training at Georgetown University and practicing conventional medicine for 15 years, he transitioned into functional and integrative care to better address the root causes of chronic illness. He is the founder of The Center for Natural & Integrative Medicine in Orlando, where he combines conventional medicine with holistic approaches to support long-term wellness. Dr. Kalidas emphasizes immune balance, environmental health, and patient-centered care in his practice. Dr. Kalidas advises the Avicenna team on integrative care.

Navid Vahidi, MD, PhD, FACS

Ophthalmologist, Advisor

Dr. Navid Vahidi is a board-certified eye physician and surgeon with extensive experience in the medical and surgical management of complex eye conditions. He serves as Chair of Ophthalmology at Orlando Regional Health System and is the Director of Central Florida Eye Associates. Dr. Vahidi earned dual B.S. degrees in Chemistry and Biology from UCLA, followed by an M.D./Ph.D. in Biophysics from the University of Illinois. He completed his surgical internship at UCSF and ophthalmology residency at the University of Chicago. He specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of glaucoma, a condition research has associated with insulin resistance and metabolic dysfunction. Dr. Vahidi advises the team on eye conditions linked to insulin resistance and metabolic health.
