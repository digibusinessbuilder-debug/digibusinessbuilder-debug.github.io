Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use the Avicenna Functional Medicine website, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader or other assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

What we’ve done

We last reviewed this website on October 15, 2026. That review covered text contrast, descriptions for images, page headings, keyboard use and a “Skip to content” link, and we fixed the problems it found.

Pages we don’t control

Booking and the client portal are provided by Practice Better, and some products link to other companies’ websites. We can’t fix problems on those sites directly, but we can help you another way.

Report a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, please tell us. We’ll work to fix it, or get you the information or service another way.
