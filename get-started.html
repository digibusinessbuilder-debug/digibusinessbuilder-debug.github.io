Where we see patients

We see patients located in Florida and Texas. Your care is overseen by a clinician licensed in the state where you are at the time of your visit: in Florida, Dr. Fawad Ahmed or Sonia Mirza, APRN; in Texas, Sonia Mirza, APRN, who is licensed in Texas. Visits are with our health coach, under that clinician’s supervision.

Payment: cash-pay, with HSA and FSA accepted

We are a cash-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. You may use an HSA, FSA or FLEX account to pay for your visit. See our prices. You have the right to a Good Faith Estimate of your costs before your care.
