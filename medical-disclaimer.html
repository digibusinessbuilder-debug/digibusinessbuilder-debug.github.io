Who we are

Avicenna Functional Medicine is operated by AIM Microbiome, LLC in Florida and Texas. Our programs may include services provided by health coaches working as part of the Avicenna care team under the supervision and direction of a licensed healthcare practitioner.

Who provides your care

Health coaches

Health coaches provide education, lifestyle and nutrition support, goal setting, accountability, and assistance implementing care plans established or approved by the supervising healthcare practitioner. Health coaches do not independently diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medications, order or independently interpret medical tests, or independently establish or modify medical treatment plans.

Licensed clinicians

Medical evaluation, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, laboratory interpretation, prescribing, and other services requiring a professional license are provided or supervised, as appropriate, by licensed healthcare practitioners acting within their respective scopes of practice.

About the information on this site

Information provided on Avicenna.clinic, including articles, videos, newsletters, educational materials, and other general content, is for educational and informational purposes and is not a substitute for individualized medical advice.

Results and medications

Individual results vary, and no particular health outcome is guaranteed. Patients should not discontinue or modify prescription medications or treatment prescribed by another healthcare professional without consulting an appropriately licensed healthcare practitioner.

Not for emergencies: call 911

Avicenna Functional Medicine is not an emergency medical service. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or seek immediate emergency medical care.

* Our approach complements not replaces conventional medical care. Always consult your physician or qualified healthcare provider before making changes to medications, treatment, or care plan. Read our full Medical Disclaimer.
