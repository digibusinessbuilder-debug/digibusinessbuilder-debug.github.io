Questions about cost

What does the discovery call cost?

Nothing. It’s a free 30-minute call to talk about your health and whether our approach is a good fit for you.

Do you take insurance?

No. Avicenna is a cash-pay practice and does not bill insurance. You may use an HSA, FSA or FLEX account.

Can I get a superbill to send to my insurance?

We aren’t able to provide superbills.

Are testing and supplements included?

No. Testing and supplements are priced separately. Testing is chosen for your situation, so its cost depends on which tests you need. We’ll tell you the cost before anything is ordered.

You have the right to a written Good Faith Estimate of your costs when you schedule care or whenever you ask. Prices are for patients located in Florida and Texas, and may change; the estimate you receive is the one that applies to you.
