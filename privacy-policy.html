1. Introduction

AIM Microbiome, LLC (“AIM Microbiome”), doing business as Avicenna Functional Medicine (“Avicenna,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operates the website Avicenna.clinic (the “Website”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our Website and use our services. By using the Website, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us

We collect the information you choose to give us through this Website:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), your reason for contacting us and how you heard about us
  • Newsletter and free-guide signups: your email address, and your first name where the form asks for it

Please don’t send medical details through the contact form or by email. Booking, intake and anything about your health happen in our client portal (see Practice Better below).

How we use and protect the health information you share as a patient is described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Log Data & Technical Data

When you access our Website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages of our Website you visit
  • Time and date of your visit
  • Device type, operating system, and other technical information

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This Website doesn’t set cookies or use tracking or analytics tools of its own. When you subscribe to our newsletter, your browser connects to Mailchimp, and when you book or sign in, to Practice Better; those services may use cookies under their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the collected information for various purposes, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Website
  • Improve your user experience
  • Reply to your messages and help you book a visit
  • Send you our newsletter if you subscribe (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities to improve the Website
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or security breaches

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. However, we may share your information in the following ways:

Service Providers

We use these service providers to run the Website:

  • Formspree receives contact form messages and forwards them to us by email.
  • Mailchimp stores newsletter subscribers and sends the newsletter.
  • Practice Better runs booking, intake and our client portal.
  • Vercel hosts the Website and keeps its request logs.

We keep contact form messages and newsletter data only as long as we need them for the purposes in this policy, or as the law requires. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time, and you can ask us to delete your contact or newsletter information by emailing us; we’ll do so unless the law requires us to keep it.

Legal Requirements

If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Avicenna or others.

Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. Data Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially reasonable means to protect your personal information. This includes:

  • Encryption of data in transit (the Website is served only over HTTPS)
  • Access to messages and subscriber lists limited to staff who need it

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Website is not directed to children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it promptly. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Third-Party Links

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites or services that we do not operate. We are not responsible for their content, privacy policies, or practices. If you visit any external websites, you do so at your own risk and should check their privacy policies.

8. Testimonials and User Content

We publish a patient testimonial only with that patient’s signed written authorization, and only the details the authorization covers. If you post a review or comment elsewhere, please remember that anything you share there may become public.

9. Affiliate and Advertising Disclosures

We take part in affiliate programs: when you buy through some of our links, such as our partner supplement shop and the products we recommend, we earn a commission. We disclose this where the links appear.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised policy on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date. Your continued use of the Website after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.
