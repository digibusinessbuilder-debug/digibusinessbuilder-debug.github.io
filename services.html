Functional Medicine Services by Telehealth

Whether you've seen many doctors or are just starting to explore functional medicine, Avicenna looks for the factors that may be contributing to your symptoms and builds a plan around them, alongside the care you already receive.

How visits and payment work

We do not take insurance. You may use your HSA, FSA, or FLEX health savings account to pay for this service. All appointments are virtual (televisits); Avicenna Functional Medicine does not have a physical clinic location.

Conditions We Help With

Autoimmune and inflammatory conditions

Autoimmune Conditions

For lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, Sjögren's and related conditions, we look at factors that may contribute, such as gut health, environmental exposures and immune regulation, alongside the care you already receive.

Book Now

Inflammatory Bowel Disease

For Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis and microscopic colitis, we look at environmental and microbiome factors that may contribute to ongoing inflammation, working alongside your gastroenterologist.

Book Now

Mast Cell Activation Syndrome

We work to identify possible triggers of mast cell reactions and build a plan to help you manage them, alongside your allergist or immunologist.

Book Now

Fibromyalgia

A personalized plan that looks at nutrient status, gut health and nervous system regulation, which may influence pain sensitivity and fatigue.

Book Now

Digestive conditions

SIBO, SIFO & IMO

Targeted testing looks for bacterial or fungal overgrowth in the small intestine. If it's found, your clinician builds a treatment plan and checks progress at follow-up visits.

Book Now

Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)

Functional testing looks for factors that may be contributing to your IBS symptoms, to guide a personalized plan.

Book Now

Dyspepsia & GERD

For reflux and indigestion, we look for possible contributors such as H. pylori, food triggers and gut imbalance. Don't stop acid-reducing medication without talking to your prescriber.

Book Now

Gut Barrier Concerns

Testing that looks at intestinal barrier function (sometimes called "leaky gut"), and a personalized plan to support gut health based on the results.

Book Now

Metabolic and hormonal health

Metabolic Syndrome & Fatty Liver

We assess insulin resistance, fatty liver and cardiometabolic risk together, with testing that looks at inflammation and the microbiome as possible contributors.

Book Now

PCOS

We look beyond irregular cycles at insulin resistance, inflammation and nutrient status, and build a plan to support hormone health.

Book Now

Kidney Support

Testing and guidance on diet, lifestyle and environmental exposures for people focused on kidney health, coordinated with your nephrologist.

Book Now

Post-viral, fatigue and nervous system conditions

Long COVID

We evaluate lingering post-viral symptoms across immune, metabolic and nervous system function, and build a plan around what we find.

Book Now

Chronic Fatigue Syndrome

We look at energy metabolism, mitochondrial function and immune regulation as possible contributors to persistent fatigue.

Book Now

Gut-Heart Axis: Palpitations & POTS

If palpitations or POTS symptoms continue after a heart evaluation, we look at how gut health and nervous system regulation may play a role. New or severe heart symptoms need urgent medical care.

Book Now

Brain & Nervous System

For neurologic and cognitive symptoms, we consider inflammation, mitochondrial health and the gut-brain axis, alongside the care you receive from your neurologist.
